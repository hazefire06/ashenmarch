# Controls compared with Myth II (1998)

A design reference for Phase 11, written in Phase 10 and brought up to date
when Phase 11 added what was missing. It compares control schemes only;
nothing from that game (names, text, art) goes into Ashenmarch.

Myth II's defaults come from the 1999 manual, which covers both PC and Mac
(transcription:
<https://oldgamesdownload.com/manual/myth-ii-soulblighter-windows-manual-english/>,
page numbers are the manual's). Behaviour that patches added (Project Magma
1.4: <https://www.projectmagma.net/downloads/myth2_143/1.4userguide.php>) is
marked as such. The Neoseeker FAQ
(<https://www.neoseeker.com/myth-ii/faqs/83594-soulblighter.html>) agrees
with the manual where they overlap.

**Mac vs PC in Myth II.** Myth was built for the one-button Mac mouse. Where
the PC uses a right click (formation facing, sending troops from the overhead
map), the Mac uses Option+click. The group modifier is Alt on PC and Command
on Mac. Ctrl+click attacks the ground on both.

| Action | Binding | Myth II source |

| Orbit around a point | Q / E | p. 24 |
| Overhead map | Tab | p. 23 |

| Select / add to selection / box select | Click / Shift+click / drag | pp. 26–27 |
| Select every unit of a type | Double-click | p. 26 |
| Formations | 1–0, the same ten in the same order (our 9 Wedge is their V-shaped Vanguard) | p. 31 |
| Special ability | T | pp. 31–32 |
| Attack the ground | Ctrl+click (Cmd+click on our Mac build, where Ctrl+click is a right click) | p. 29 |
| Scoreboard | F7 | p. 26 |
| Edge scrolling | Optional | pp. 20, 25 |
| Wheel zoom | Mouse wheel (Myth II: patch 1.4, with "Use Direct Input") | Magma 1.4 |
| Stop | Space (Ashenmarch since Phase 10) | pp. 23, 29 |
| Center the camera on the selection | H (Ashenmarch since Phase 10) | p. 25 |

## Different, and the Classic preset (Phase 11)

Phase 11 added a Classic preset (Settings > Controls) beside the old scheme, now called Modern, which stays the default.

| Action | Myth II | Modern | Classic |
|---|---|---|---|
| Move or attack with units selected | Left click on the ground or an enemy (p. 28) | Right click | Left click (on your own unit it selects) |
| Strafe the camera | Z / X (p. 24) | A / D | Z / X |
| Turn the camera in place | A / D (p. 25) | Z / X | A / D |
| Zoom | C in, V out (p. 25) | V in, C out | C in, V out |
| Save a group | Hold Alt+digit (Cmd on Mac) for a second or two (p. 27) | Cmd/Ctrl+digit | Hold the group key+digit (Cmd on Mac, Alt elsewhere) 0.8 s |
| Recall a group | Alt+digit (Cmd on Mac) (p. 27) | Option/Alt+digit | Tap the group key+digit |
| Pause | Esc pauses and opens the menu (p. 22) | Esc opens the pause menu too; P pauses without it | the same |

Everything Myth II had that Ashenmarch didn't, in both presets:

| Action | Myth II binding | Ashenmarch | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Guard: melee holds, ranged fires at anything in range and repositions if attacked | G | G | pp. 23–24, 30 |
| Scatter | B | B | p. 30 |
| Retreat | R | R: the group falls back 15 m from the nearest enemy, facing it | p. 30 |
| Waypoints and patrol (up to 4; Shift+click the first point again for a loop, the last for back and forth) | Shift+click on the ground | Shift + the order click (patrols fight) | p. 29 |
| Formation facing | Right-drag (Option-drag on Mac) | the same | pp. 25, 30–31 |
| Rotate the formation, even while moving | Left / Right arrow | the same | p. 30 |

## In Ashenmarch only

- Attack-move (Cmd/Ctrl+right click). The manual has no such order: Myth II
  units engage only what comes into range or what they are told to attack.
- The mouse-only control bar.
- P to pause without the menu.
- Every key can be rebound (Settings > Controls, Phase 10), and each preset
  keeps its own rebinds.
- An Xbox pad (Phase 11): a cursor on the left stick, wheels for formations
  and orders on the shoulders, every menu by focus.

## How close

With the Classic preset, every one of Myth II's default bindings above has
its match, apart from the game speed's top (4x here, 16x there). Modern keeps
the four differences that shaped its feel: the order button on the right,
strafe and turn swapped, zoom the other way, and two group keys instead of
hold-to-save.

## Caveats in the sources

- The manual's own volume keys contradict each other (p. 26: the text says F3
  louder and F4 quieter, the key diagram the opposite). Ashenmarch doesn't use
  F3 or F4.
- Two sources count nine formations, not ten. The manual lists ten, with 0 =
  Circle, and is the authority.
