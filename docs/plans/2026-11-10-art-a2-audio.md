# Art track A2: audio — implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Replace Phase 10's 16 placeholder sounds with a real sound bank. Make the light side bark, in text and then in voice. Add ambience, marching and Riverside's two-layer music, and keep audio within 270 MB of Web tab memory.

**Architecture:**
- **One bank.** Every sound is a cue in `audio-src/cues.toml` (or a voice file). `process.py build` turns the picked takes, or a migrated placeholder, into a JSON sidecar. A headless Godot builder turns the sidecar into `data/audio/sound_bank.tres` and `barks.tres`.
- **Existing nodes, extended.** Phase 10's `SfxPlayer`, `Ambience` and `UiSounds` read the bank, and a hand-written `SoundMap` says which cue each unit type and projectile makes. `Barks` and `Music` are new nodes beside them.
- **Web memory.** On Web, the one-shots a launch can need are registered at mission load. Loops stream. The budget check computes audio's share and holds it to its own cap.
- **Paid steps.** `generate.py` is the only code that spends credits, and Tim runs it. `process.py` is free and does the trimming, levelling, looping, audition pages and picks.

**Tech stack:**
- Godot 4.7.2 and GDScript, tested with GUT.
- Python 3.12, standard library only (`tomllib`, `urllib`, `wave`, `array`), tested with `unittest`. ffmpeg.
- The ElevenLabs HTTP API.
- Chrome through the chrome-devtools MCP, for the Web measurements.

**Spec:** `docs/specs/2026-11-09-art-a2-audio-design.md` (approved 2026-11-10). Read it with this plan. Its §4 (secrets) and §8.4 (lines, the originality rule, the clan line) come from `docs/specs/2026-10-01-art-audio-design.md`, and still hold.

## Review Focus

The spec implies these five cases, but its test list doesn't pin them. Each is pinned by a test in the task named.

1. **A cue with no sound yet.** This happens before SFX are bought, for a type with no death cue of its own, or for a cue a hand edit renamed. It plays nothing and warns once per cue, never every frame. A type without its own cue uses its nature's fallback. Tests: Task 2 `test_a_missing_cue_is_silent_and_warns_once`, and Task 5 `test_a_death_cue_the_bank_lacks_falls_back_by_nature`.
2. **A dead or missing speaker.** A unit can die, despawn or be id 0 between its event and its sound or bark. That gives no sound and no bark, and never a crash. Tests: Task 6 `test_events_about_missing_units_are_skipped`, and Task 14 `test_a_kill_by_a_dead_or_missing_attacker_makes_no_bark`.
3. **The viewer switches side** (the debug side switch, or a Dark skirmish). Hidden-unit silence, music counts and barks all follow the new viewer. The undead never bark, so a Dark viewer hears no barks. Tests: Task 6 `test_the_viewer_follows_side_changed`, and Task 14 `test_barks_are_silent_when_the_viewer_is_dark`.
4. **Many steps in one frame:** a replay at 8×, or a catch-up after a hitch. Each step's events still play within the per-tick caps, and the barks don't chain past the one-at-a-time rule, because their clock doesn't move inside a frame. Test: Task 14 `test_many_steps_in_one_frame_give_at_most_one_bark`.
5. **A hand-edited or hostile input** (the repo is public): an id such as `../x` or `Hit`, a pick for an unknown cue or an out-of-range take, a planted symlink under `assets/audio/` or `audio-src/raw/`. It's refused before anything is written or bought. Tests: Task 3 `test_ids_that_are_not_safe_file_names_are_refused`, Task 17 `test_picks_with_an_unknown_id_or_take_change_nothing` and `test_a_planted_symlink_stops_the_build`, and Task 16 `test_a_planted_symlink_stops_a_download`.

## Changes from the spec, decided while planning

- **`cues.toml` and `process.py build` come in the foundation.** The bank builder needs a sidecar to migrate the 16 placeholders, so each cue an old sound fits gets a `placeholder` field. The paid parts, the audition page and picks still come at step 4.
- **The beds, marching and `Music` code come before step 2.** Step 2 measures streamed audio under load, so all 12 streamed players have to exist and run, on synthetic loops. Only the bought loops and music wait for steps 7 and 8.
- **`SelectionController` gains `player_selected(ids)`.** `UnitSelection.changed` doesn't fire when the same unit is clicked again, which the annoyed counter needs. It also fires when a selected unit dies, which isn't a selection the player made. Select barks and `ui_select` listen to `player_selected`. Group recall keeps `group_changed`.
- **Order kinds.** A move order onto an enemy unit can't be told apart: the controller sends it as a ground move, and there's no unit-target command. So ATTACK covers attack-move, ground attack, and attack route points.
  - MOVE: move, patrol, move route points, and interact (pick up, use).
  - SPECIAL: special, and heal.
  - RETREAT: retreat.
  - OTHER: stop, guard, scatter, turn in place, and the arrow-key reissue.
- **Fallback sounds by nature are entries in the sound map,** not extra cues. `_living` uses the villager's death cue, and `_undead` uses the Husk's death and groan. The 54-cue list stays as the spec has it, and nothing extra is bought.
- **Distance limits.** Event barks (kill, hurt, death, status, fizzle and heal-on-undead) come only from units within 160 m of the focus, so a bark nobody can hear isn't charged against the side-wide gap.
- **`ui_deny` on `CANT_REACH`** plays only for the viewer's own units.
- **Where barks come from.** Barks play in replays for event triggers. Order and selection triggers can't arise there.
- **The speaker "nearest the screen centre"** is the unit nearest the camera's ground focus, which is the screen centre's ground point.
- **The victory speaker** is the surviving viewer-side unit nearest the focus whose voice file has victory lines.
- **`LICENSES.md` audio rows** are generated by `process.py build`, between two marker comments.
- **`scripts/art/manifest.py` takes an allowlist parameter,** so `generate.py` reuses its safe, resumable manifest. Art's own allowlist and tests are unchanged, and the security review (Task 18) covers the change.
- **Body sounds.** Only the Stormcaller's body is `dry` (spec §7.1). Every other type is `flesh`, and Tim can flip any type in `sound_map.tres` at the listening build.

  - `SoundCue extends Resource`, with these exports:
    - `id: StringName`;
    - `category: StringName`, one of `melee`, `projectile`, `death`, `groan`, `ui`, `march`, `bed` or `music`;
    - `streams: Array[AudioStream]`;
    - `bus: StringName = &"Effects"`;
    - `level_db: float = 0.0`;
    - `pitch_spread: float = 0.06`;
    - `max_voices: int = 3`;

    - `playback: AudioServer.PlaybackType = AudioServer.PLAYBACK_TYPE_DEFAULT`;
    - `file_bytes: int = 0`, the imported files' bytes, for the budget.
  - `SoundBank extends Resource`:
    - `const PATH: String = "res://data/audio/sound_bank.tres"`;
    - `@export var cues: Array[SoundCue]`;
    - `func cue(id: StringName) -> SoundCue`: the cue, or null with one `push_warning` per id per bank;
    - `func declares(id: StringName) -> bool`, with no warning;
    - `func has(id: StringName) -> bool`: declared, with at least one stream, and no warning;
    - `func pick(id: StringName, rng: RandomNumberGenerator) -> AudioStream`: a random variant, or null;
    - `func ids() -> Array[StringName]`;
    - `func warned() -> Array[StringName]`, for tests;
    - `static func shared() -> SoundBank`: loaded from PATH once per session and held in a static var, never freed (spec §4.3.1). It returns an empty bank, with one warning, if PATH is missing.
  - `AudioFixtures` (tests only):
    - `static func silent(seconds: float, rate: int = 8000) -> AudioStreamWAV`: 16-bit mono zeros;
    - `static func bank(variants: Dictionary, max_voices: int = 3, seconds: float = 0.2) -> SoundBank`: each id (StringName key) with that many silent streams of `seconds`. It's an untyped Dictionary, so test literals pass straight in. Pool tests use `seconds = 5.0`, so the voices are still playing when they're checked.

    - `save_voice(name: str, description: str, generated_voice_id: str) -> str`: the new `voice_id`, checked.
    - `speech(voice_id: str, text: str, model_id: str) -> bytes`;
    - `music(prompt: str, length_ms: int, model_id: str) -> bytes`.
    - Every audio reply must have an `AUDIO_TYPES` content type, and audio bytes that start with `RIFF` or an MP3 frame or ID3 header. Anything else is an `ElevenLabsError`.

### Task 17: process.py: trim, level, loop, audition, picks and licences

**Files:**
- Create: `scripts/audio/dsp.py`, `ffmpeg_io.py`, `audition.py`, `picks.py`, each with a `test_*.py`
- Modify:
  - `scripts/audio/process.py`: `batch`, `picks`, and `build` writing finished assets, `.import` files and `LICENSES.md` rows;

**Interfaces:**
- Produces:
  - `dsp.py`, on `array('h')` interleaved 16-bit frames:
    - `trim(frames, channels: int, rate: int, threshold_db: float = -50.0, tail_fade_ms: float = 30.0) -> array`;
    - `peak_normalise(frames, peak_db: float = -1.0) -> array`;
    - `loop_crossfade(frames, channels: int, rate: int, fade_s: float = 1.0) -> array`: equal power, folding the tail's last `fade_s` into the head; the output is `len - fade_frames`;
    - `match_length(a, b, channels: int) -> tuple[array, array]`: both cut to the shorter.
  - `ffmpeg_io.py`:
    - `require_ffmpeg() -> None`;
    - `decode(path: Path, rate: int, channels: int) -> array`;
    - `write_wav(frames, rate: int, channels: int, path: Path) -> None`;
    - `write_ogg(frames, rate: int, channels: int, path: Path, quality: int = 5) -> None`;
    - `loudness_normalise(frames, rate: int, channels: int, lufs: float) -> array`, using the ffmpeg `loudnorm` filter.
    - Starting targets: barks −18 LUFS, beds and music −23 LUFS. One-shots are peaked to −1 dBFS, then mixed by `level_db`.
  - `audition.py`: `page(batch: str, takes: list[Take]) -> str`.
    - `Take(id: str, take: int, prompt_or_text: str, file: str, voice: str | None)`.
    - The page is self-contained HTML, with every string HTML-escaped and no external scripts. Each take has an `<audio controls>` and a checkbox. "Save picks" downloads `picks-<batch>.json` from a Blob.
    - The JSON is `{"batch", "cues": {id: [takes]}}`, `{"batch", "lines": {id: {voice: take}}}`, or `{"batch", "design": {voice: n}}`.
  - `picks.py`:
    - `apply_cue_picks(text: str, picks: dict[str, list[int]]) -> str`, and `apply_line_picks(text: str, picks: dict[str, dict[str, int]]) -> str`;
    - each replaces only the `picks = …` line inside its `[cue.<id>]` or `[line.<id>]` table, leaving every other byte as it was;
    - each refuses a table with no picks line.
  - `process.py`:
    - `batch <name>`, where the name is a cue category, `loops`, `music`, `voices-<unit>`, `design-<unit>` or `ab`. It processes every raw take of the batch into `audio-src/audition/<name>/`, and writes `audio-src/audition/<name>.html`.
    - `picks <file>` validates the file against the cue book or voice files: every id exists, takes are within 1..takes, voices belong to the unit. Then it rewrites the TOML. With any error it writes nothing.
    - `build`, for each picked take:
      - one-shots: trimmed, peaked, mono 16-bit WAV at `assets/audio/sfx/<cue>_<n>.wav`, with a `.import` of `compress/mode=2` and `edit/loop_mode=0`;
      - barks: trimmed, normalised, mono Ogg at `assets/audio/voice/<voice>/<line>.ogg`;
      - loops: crossfaded stereo Ogg at `assets/audio/loop/<cue>.ogg`, with `.import` `loop=true`;
      - music: both layers cut to the same length, at `assets/audio/music/<layer>.ogg`.
    - `build` then writes the sidecar (Task 4), and rewrites the block between `<!-- audio:begin -->` and `<!-- audio:end -->` in `assets/LICENSES.md`: one row per shipped file, with its source (ElevenLabs, its endpoint and model), its terms ("ElevenLabs paid plan: commercial use, no attribution"), the date and the cue or line id.
    - Any symlink under `assets/audio/` or `audio-src/raw/` stops it before it writes.
    - Each `.import` file is written only if it's missing or its `[params]` differ, so Godot's uid lines survive.
