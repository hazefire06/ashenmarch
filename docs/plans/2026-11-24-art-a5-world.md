# Art track A5, Phase 1: the look — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Replace the blocky placeholder world with textured, smooth-shaded ground, real CC0 building and wall models on the sim's existing footprints, a water surface, and re-graded atmospheres, without changing the sim.

**Architecture:**
- The map generators record what they build: structures and the water surface. They save it as view-only files beside each map, and the height and mask files stay byte-identical.
- `TerrainView` blends a CC0 texture array by smoothly filtered ground weights, with smooth vertex normals.
- A new `WaterView` draws the water surface, and a new `StructuresView` places kit models fitted to each structure's blocked samples. One shared model shader carries the grade, the weather and fire.

**Tech Stack:** Godot 4.7, GDScript and Godot shaders (Compatibility renderer), GUT; Python 3 for the asset import script; CC0 sources (ambientCG, Poly Haven, Quaternius, Kenney).

**Spec:** `docs/specs/2026-11-24-art-a5-world-design.md` (approved by Tim, 2026-11-24). Read it first. Phase 2 (the play) is out of scope here.

## Global Constraints

- **View only.**
  - No edits to `sim/` or `data/units/`, nor to the map files the sim reads: `maps/*/height.png`, `maps/*/mask.png` and `maps/*/<map>.tres` (`MapInfo`).
  - `make maps` must leave those files byte-identical (verified 2026-11-24 at b97cbab), and `make verify-replays` must match `main`.
- **Assets are owned or CC0 only.** Sources: ambientCG, Poly Haven, Quaternius and Kenney (all CC0, verified 2026-11-24).
  - Every shipped file under `assets/` gets a row in `assets/LICENSES.md`.
  - `assets/` and `art-src/` are LFS. Godot skips `art-src/`.
- **Downloads are untrusted.** Each archive is unpacked into its own new, empty folder, and only images (`.png`, `.jpg`) and glTF (`.gltf`, `.glb`, `.bin`) are taken. If a pack can't be fetched without an account, stop and ask Tim to download it into `art-src/world/`. Never switch sources silently.
- **Fit.** Each model edge lies within 0.5 m of the edge of its structure's blocked samples, which win over the generator's nominal rectangle. A model's top is the structure's sim height: houses peak at `height_m` above their base, and the mill at 6 m.
- **GDScript** is statically typed everywhere, one class per file, with `class_name`. `@warning_ignore` only with a comment.
- **GUT:** never pass `-d`, and read the output (`Totals` and `[Failed]`), not the exit code. Run one file with `-gconfig= -gtest=res://tests/view/<file>.gd`.
- **Renderer:** `gl_compatibility` on every platform, so there is no depth or screen texture in shaders.
- **No paid steps** in Phase 1 (no Meshy, no ElevenLabs).
- **No screen captures, osascript or System Events.** Browser checks go through the chrome-devtools MCP only.
- **Web budget:** the `?budget=1` line must stay `measured` with the tab ≤ 1000 MB PASS, and audio still ≤ 270 PASS.
- **Commits:** an imperative one-line summary, a body explaining why, and the trailer `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`. Stage files by explicit path.

## Review Focus

The five failure modes most likely to bite a player that no task's main test covers. Each has a pinned test in the task named.

1. **An explosion scars the ground beside or under a house.** `rebuild_region` must keep the house's covered samples at their base height, with smooth normals across the chunk edge, and no slab poking through. (Task 2 test `test_a_scar_under_a_structure_keeps_its_ground_hidden`.)
2. **A terrain with no `structures.tres` or `water.png`** (the sandbox, the test terrains, a future map) must draw textured ground with no models and no water, and no error. (Task 8 test `test_a_map_without_world_files_still_builds`.)
3. **Water touching a structure** (a house stamped on wet ground warns today): no water quad may be drawn over a covered sample. (Task 5 test `test_no_water_over_a_structure`.)
4. **The Ford's palisade ring** (96 points, diagonal runs, a gate gap): every blocked wall sample must be covered by a placed piece, and every piece must stay within 0.5 m of the wall's blocked band. (Task 6 test `test_the_fords_palisade_is_covered_and_fits`.)
5. **Fire spreading into a house** must make that house glow while its samples burn and stay charred after. (Task 6 test `test_a_house_glows_while_its_footprint_burns_then_stays_charred`.)

### Task 4: The textured ground

**Files:**
- Create: `view/terrain/world_textures.gd`
- Modify: `view/terrain/terrain.gdshader`, `view/terrain/terrain_view.gd`
- Test: `tests/view/test_terrain_view.gd`, `tests/view/test_terrain_grade.gd`

**Interfaces:**
- Produces `WorldTextures`:
  - `const LAYERS: Array[StringName] = [&"grass", &"brush", &"forest", &"sand", &"rock", &"mud"]`;
  - `static func albedo() -> Texture2DArray` and `static func normal() -> Texture2DArray`. Each loads `res://assets/world/textures/ground_{albedo,normal}.png`, or builds a 6-layer, 4 × 4 solid fallback when the file is absent. The fallback albedo uses `TerrainPalette.GROUND_TINTS` plus a mud brown; the fallback normal is flat.
- Produces the shader uniforms that `TerrainView` sets:
  - `ground_albedo` and `ground_normal` (`sampler2DArray`);
  - `ground_weights_a`: linear-filtered RGBA, one texel per sample, holding the grass, brush, wood and sand weights;
  - `ground_weights_b`: linear-filtered RGBA, holding R rock, G mud, B covered (1 under a structure), A unused;
  - `detail_noise`: a 256 × 256 tiling noise image made once from `FastNoiseLite`;
  - `tile_m: float = 3.0`, `border_jitter: float = 0.35`, `rock_normal_y: float = 0.707` (45°, the walkable limit) and `rock_band: float = 0.1`.
  - `height_ramp` and `ground_tint` are no longer used by the 3D shader. The overhead map keeps `TerrainPalette`.
- Kept uniforms, unchanged: `water_tint`, `blocked_map`, `blocked_tint`, `fire_state`, `wetness`, `snow_cover`, `grade_tint`, `grade_desaturation`, `ground_recolor[5]`, `water_grade`, `cell_size` and `sample_count`.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the failing tests.**
  - `test_ground_weights_sum_to_one_on_every_sample`: on a test terrain mixing all five grounds and some water, `a.r + a.g + a.b + a.a + b.r + b.g` is 1 ±0.01 per texel.
  - `test_mud_is_weighted_at_the_shore`: a dry sample within 2 m of a depth-1 sample has a mud weight above 0, and one 5 m away has 0.
  - `test_the_textured_ground_receives_every_kept_uniform`: after `build` with fire, weather and `set_grade`, each kept uniform is non-null and equals what was set.
  - `test_covered_samples_skip_the_blocked_darkening`: `ground_weights_b` has B = 1 on a structure's samples and 0 elsewhere.
  - `test_world_textures_fall_back_without_assets`: `WorldTextures.albedo().get_layers() == 6`.
  - Update `test_terrain_grade.gd` so it asserts the grade uniforms reach the new material. Delete its `height_ramp` and `ground_tint` assertions.
- [ ] **Step 2: Run the two files.** Expected: failures.
- [ ] **Step 3: Implement the shader.**
  - **vertex():** sets `TANGENT` along +x and `BINORMAL` along +z projected onto the surface.
  - **Weights:**
    - Each layer's weight is sampled linearly.
    - Its border is shifted by `detail_noise` × `border_jitter`.
    - Rock is added where `NORMAL.y < rock_normal_y`, blending over `rock_band`.
    - The weights are renormalised to sum to 1.
  - **Sampling:**
    - Each layer is sampled at `world_pos.xz / tile_m`.
    - Rock is triplanar where steep.
    - The tint is varied gently by a low-frequency read of `detail_noise`.
  - **Order of the kept layers:**
    1. the grade recolour per ground, weighted by the weights, then desaturation and tint;
    2. fire;
    3. wetness;
    4. snow;
    5. the water tint;
    6. the blocked darkening, only where `blocked_map` is 1 and covered is 0.
- [ ] **Step 4: Run the two files, then the full GUT suite.** Expected: all pass.
- [ ] **Step 5: Run a Web smoke check.**
