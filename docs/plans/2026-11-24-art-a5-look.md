# Art track A5, Phase 1: the look Tim settled

The values the world look build ended on, and why. Spec: `docs/specs/2026-11-24-art-a5-world-design.md`. Plan: `docs/plans/2026-11-24-art-a5-world.md`. Each value is the default in the file named, so a later tuning pass starts here.

## Tim's verdict (2026-11-27)

"Still think the world art can look better but I'm ok with closing it for now."

Phase 1 is accepted as it stands. Tim still wants more work on the look, and that work is listed under "Next" below, beside Phase 2.

## The decisions, with Tim's reasons

### The ground (2026-11-25)

- **What happened.** The first look build drew the textured ground, but the creek was a hard, stair-stepped band. The cause was the water tint, which was still sampled nearest. After that was fixed, Tim said the ground was "still weird".
- **The diagnosis.** At the default zoom:
  - the 3 m tiles repeated as stripes and stipple inside each patch;
  - each material was one flat tone, inside blobs with stencil-cut edges.
- **The fix:**
  - anti-tiling: each layer is read at two scales, the far one turned and offset;
  - macro variation in value, hue and saturation over about 30 m blobs;
  - soft borders, 3–4 m gradients bent at two frequencies, with a brightness-as-height blend.
- **Tim's verdict:** "ground looks better".
- **Values:**
  - `view/terrain/terrain.gdshader`:
    - `tile_m` 3.0, `far_scale` 3.7, `far_angle` 0.61 rad;
    - `far_near_m` 12, `far_far_m` 50, `far_share` 0.75, `far_noise` 0.35;
    - `macro_m` 160, `macro_value` 0.12, `macro_hue` 0.04, `macro_saturation` 0.12;
    - `border_width` 1.5 samples, `bend_large` 2.5 every 90 m, `bend_fine` 0.6 every 6 m;
    - `height_blend` 0.8 (lower it towards 0.4 for crisper "dirt between blades" edges);
    - `border_jitter` 0.35;
    - `rock_normal_y` 0.707 with `rock_band` 0.1. Faces steeper than 45° turn to triplanar rock, so Old Mill's cliff reads as rock.
  - Mud reaches 3 m from the water.

### Scatter

- **Tim's request:** "needs trees and stuff".
- **His choice:** view-only ground scatter now. Grass tufts, ferns and plants, flowers, small rocks and low bushes are all walkable, and none of them is in the sim. Solid standing trees, downed-tree cover and choke points go to Phase 2, because they block arrows and movement and so are gameplay. Phase 2 starts after this merges.
- **Values** (`view/scatter/`):
  - Placement is seeded from the map's folder name, so campaign, skirmish and replay all show the same scatter.
  - Scatter keeps off structures by each item's footprint radius, and off worn trails.
  - `FADE_END_M` is measured from the camera's look point: grass 65 m, plants 50, bushes 70, pebbles 60, reeds 70.
  - Nothing is drawn once the camera is more than 200 m from its look point.
  - `MAX_SPAN_M` caps each item's width: grass and plants 0.8 m, bushes 1.2, pebbles 1.0, reeds 0.8. This keeps every plant smaller than a soldier.
  - `DENSITY_SCALE_WEB` 0.4.

### The reference look (2026-11-27)

- **What it was built from:** a Myth II gameplay video, used for **principles only**. No layout, art, name or text was taken from it.
- **Tim's choice:** do it now, before the world art merges.
- **What it changed:**
  - **Continuous dark grass.** Brush and forest are darker shades of one grass rather than separate materials. Sand shows only within 6 m of water, and inland sand reads as a faint dry grass. Values: `grass_tint` (0.52, 0.7, 0.42), `brush_tint` (0.86, 0.9, 0.8), `forest_tint` (0.68, 0.76, 0.66), `dry_tint` (1.12, 1.04, 0.8), `bed_tint` (0.45, 0.5, 0.5).
  - **Worn trails.** View-only `maps/<id>/trails.tres` files, baked into a 0.5 m mask:
    - dirt cores with broken edges, and ruts on roads 3 m or wider;
    - `trail_strength` 0.9, `trail_edge_low` 0.15, `trail_edge_high` 0.55, `trail_breakup` 0.45, `trail_rut_dark` 0.22.
  - **Reeds.** Clumps of 6–13 stems in up to 0.3 m of water and on banks within 1.5 m, gated by a 16 m clumping noise. 35% of stems carry a seed head.
  - **Darker water** (`view/terrain/water.gdshader`, `water_view.gd`):
    - deep (0.015, 0.06, 0.08), shallow (0.13, 0.22, 0.22);
    - Fresnel reflection of the mission's sky, `fresnel_strength` 0.75, power 4, floor 0.04;
    - a softened sun glint, `sun_specular` 0.3 and `sun_roughness` 0.2.
  - **A closer camera:** below.

### The camera (2026-11-27)

- **Tim's choice:** keep the default at **22 m with Godot's 75° vertical FOV**. He turned down a narrower FOV and moving closer.
- **What that costs:** at 22 m, soldiers are about 3–4% of the screen height, against about 7–8% in the reference. Tim accepted that to keep the overview.
- **Values:**
  - `RtsCamera.DEFAULT_DISTANCE` 22.
  - The pitch is piecewise: 35° at 8 m, 50° at 22 m (the angle the sprites are rendered at), 65° at 250 m.
  - Missions and skirmish maps open at 22 m.
  - Riverside opens facing the way the squad deploys.
  - The bench keeps its old 75 m camera.

### Water

Tim: "Water looked good." The Ford's shallow-ford grid was later smoothed (half-float depth, averaged reads, drowned bed relief), and the water darkened for the reference look.

### Camera limits: not part of A5

Tim can still zoom out to the edge of the map and see past it. That is **Phase 13's** work, not A5's:
- a map border band;
- edge fog;
- a 150 m zoom cap;
- a sky limit.

Per the ruling of 2026-11-27, Phase 13 does those (its T5) first, after A5 merges.

## The grades (Task 9, 2026-11-27)

The three atmospheres were tuned for flat colours. On the continuous-grass ground:
- **Old Mill's ember grade** turned the whole frame orange. Tim's standing note on it is "too orange".
- **The Ford** came out as bright as Riverside.

How the grades were checked:
- Each was judged in `make capture` shots (`.superpowers/sdd/2026-11-24-art-a5-world/capture-task9-g2`, `-g3`, `-g4`), by eye, with enlarged crops. Example crops: `trio-openings.png` and `crop-mill-before-after.png` in `capture-task9-g4`.
- Each was measured as mean luma over the play area (the HUD and control bar left out).
- Each was set against a still from the reference video (luma 49, red/green 0.91).

### Riverside (`data/atmospheres/riverside.tres`): unchanged

It is already near the reference's dark green, and Tim approved its ground and water in look builds 3 to 6.

### The Ford (`data/atmospheres/the_ford.tres`): one step darker

| Value | Was | Now |
|---|---|---|
| `terrain_tint` | (0.92, 0.95, 0.88) | (0.85, 0.88, 0.82) |
| `ambient_energy` | 0.55 | 0.5 |
| `sun_energy` | 0.6 | 0.55 |

Why: it put The Ford about 10% under Riverside, so the campaign still darkens mission by mission. Everything else (its grey-green sky and mist, 0.35 desaturation, straw grass, murky water) is unchanged.

### The order, measured

Mean luma of the play area, in the same shots:

| Shot | Riverside | The Ford | Old Mill (was) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opening | 81.6 | 72.0 | 42.7 (38.0) |
| Battle | 76.7 | 69.9 | 45.7 (41.0) |
| Close | 72.2 | 68.2 | 44.1 (40.7) |

- Old Mill's red/green ratio went from 1.67–1.94 (orange) to 1.12–1.21 (warm olive-brown). Riverside and The Ford are 0.88–0.94.
- Old Mill is a little brighter than before because the red has gone out of its light. It stays far darker than The Ford.
- The kit's terracotta roofs are their own colour and still read warm under the amber sun.

### What the tests hold

- `tests/sim/test_campaign_missions.gd` keeps the darkening order: desaturation, fog density and water tint all rise mission by mission.
- It also keeps Old Mill's ember character: the sky's red is above its green, the sun's red is 0.5 above its blue, and the fog runs red > green > blue.
- The new grade meets all of these without editing the test.
