# Controls compared with Myth II (1998)

A design reference for Phase 11, written in Phase 10. It compares control
schemes only; nothing from that game (names, text, art) goes into Ashenmarch.

Myth II's defaults come from the 1999 manual, which covers both PC and Mac
(transcription:
<https://oldgamesdownload.com/manual/myth-ii-soulblighter-windows-manual-english/>,
page numbers are the manual's). Behaviour that patches added (Project Magma
1.4: <https://www.projectmagma.net/downloads/myth2_143/1.4userguide.php>) is
marked as such. The Neoseeker FAQ
(<https://www.neoseeker.com/myth-ii/faqs/83594-soulblighter.html>) agrees
with the manual where they overlap.

**Mac vs PC in Myth II.** Myth was built for the one-button Mac mouse. Where
the PC uses a right click (formation facing, sending troops from the overhead
map), the Mac uses Option+click. The group modifier is Alt on PC and Command
on Mac. Ctrl+click attacks the ground on both.

## Different

| Action | Myth II | Ashenmarch |
|---|---|---|
| Move or attack with units selected | Left click on the ground or an enemy (p. 28) | Right click |
| Strafe the camera | Z / X (p. 24) | A / D |
| Turn the camera in place | A / D (p. 25) | Z / X |
| Zoom | C in, V out (p. 25) | V in, C out |
| Save a group | Hold Alt+digit (Cmd on Mac) for a second or two (p. 27) | Cmd/Ctrl+digit |
| Recall a group | Alt+digit (Cmd on Mac) (p. 27) | Option/Alt+digit |
| Pause | Esc pauses and opens the menu (p. 22) | Esc opens the pause menu too; P pauses without it |

## In Ashenmarch only

- Attack-move (Cmd/Ctrl+right click). The manual has no such order: Myth II
  units engage only what comes into range or what they are told to attack.
- The mouse-only control bar.
- P to pause without the menu.
- Every key can be rebound (Settings > Controls, Phase 10).

## How close

About half of Myth II's default bindings match. Selection, formations, the
special, the orbit and forward keys, the overhead map, Stop and Center
(since Phase 10) are the same. Four differences shape the feel:
- the order button: left click in Myth II, right click here;
- the strafe and turn keys are swapped;
- zoom runs the other way;
- the group modifiers differ.

Six Myth II orders don't exist here at all: guard, scatter, retreat,
waypoints and patrol, formation facing, and rotating the formation. Phase 11
adds those orders and a "Classic" control preset that closes the remaining
gaps.

## Caveats in the sources

- The manual's own volume keys contradict each other (p. 26: the text says F3
  louder and F4 quieter, the key diagram the opposite). Ashenmarch doesn't use
  F3 or F4.
- Two sources count nine formations, not ten. The manual lists ten, with 0 =
  Circle, and is the authority.
