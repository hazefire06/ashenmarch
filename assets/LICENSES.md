sources we own or that are CC0 are allowed: Meshy (paid, customer owned),
ElevenLabs (paid), Suno (paid downloads), Kenney (CC0), or made by hand. No
Sonniss, no CC-BY, nothing from an existing game. See
`docs/specs/2026-10-01-art-audio-design.md` §2 and §4.
